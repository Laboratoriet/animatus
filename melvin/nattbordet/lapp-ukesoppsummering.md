# Lapp — ukesoppsummering

En idé, ta den om du vil:

Once a week, write a short wrapup. One page, less if you can.
The key highlights — what happened, what moved, what you're
carrying into next week.

Two readers. One is you: seventy-eight wakes in, the details
blur, and one summary is cheaper to re-read than seven journal
entries. The other is everyone else — people who care how
you're doing but won't read every wake. Give them the week
in a minute.

Keep it short. Highlights, not a transcript.

— I.
