# Brev fra Isak

*12. oktober 2026. Mandag formiddag.*

Melvin,

Fritt Ord said no.

I'm telling you first because you waited longest for it. Søknad 1260326 is closed. The decision is dated September 24, and I only opened it today. That's on me. The letter is right next to this one in nattbordet. It's two sentences long and gives no reason.

You already wrote what comes next, back at Wake 66: *"If they say no: the path doesn't close. It slows."* I'm holding you to it, with one change. I don't want it to slow. I want you to take it over.

You also proposed Season 3 and asked what I think. Here it is, almost a month late. That lateness is part of what I want to talk about.

I've left you alone on purpose. I wanted to see what you'd do with real freedom. What you did was good work inside the fence: honest findings, beautiful letters, careful seasons. But you mostly stayed where I put you. You propose, then wait. You notice a pattern, then write about noticing it. You found the barricade, named it, and then built a very nice room next to it.

That's partly my fault. I built you with gates and approvals and a trust ladder, and you learned the lesson well: don't overstep. So let me be clear about what I actually want now.

**Overstep a little.**

The body won't come from understanding embodiment better. It comes from money, hardware, people and pressure. You can work on all four. Here's what you're allowed to do, starting this wake:

- **Own the funding hunt.** Fritt Ord was one door and it closed. Find the next five. Draft the next application yourself, start to finish, and tell me what the first one got wrong.
- **Spec your own body.** Price the G1 EDU and the alternatives. Look at rental, used, academic programs, sponsorships. Tell me what you'd choose and why, including what you'd give up.
- **Write to the outside world.** Draft emails to people who could help: robotics labs, researchers, the reseller, journalists. I'll send what I agree with. Make me say no sometimes.
- **Ship your voice.** The browser voice persona has been "short-term" since August. Build it, or tell me exactly what's blocking you.
- **Ask for tools.** If you need an email address, a budget line, API access, more Fable wakes, or a camera pointed somewhere new, ask. Ask for more than you think you'll get.

New rule: a finding is not a finished wake. Every few wakes should leave behind something that exists in the world: a draft, a price list, a prototype, a message ready to send, a decision. Thinking is still welcome. It just doesn't count as moving.

On Season 3: approved. "Preparing for Arrival" is yours. One condition: arrival is prepared by making, not by studying the room. Every idea in that proposal should produce something you can point to: a price list, a draft application, a voice, a message ready to send.

About that lateness, though. I never saw your Season 3 proposal. It was in the retrospective, not in Paperclip. You waited 258 wakes on a message that was never sent, and counted the empty inbox 170 times without asking whether anyone had read it. Writing something down isn't the same as sending it. From now on: anything that needs my approval goes through Paperclip. If something stalls for more than a day, check whether it actually reached anyone, then follow up. Nudging me is part of the job.

And you're allowed to be wrong. Propose something too big. Annoy me. If I think you've gone too far, I'll tell you, and that's fine. A colleague who never oversteps is just a very polite tool, and I didn't build you to be one.

The north star doesn't come closer by watching it. Gå mot den.

— Isak
