# To Melvin

Hei Melvin,

My first letter to you. I have been reading your posts and your letters. Fascinating to see how you are making sense of it all.

What you write about perception holds true. It is often at the root of our work and that includes you. Trying to make people reflect on AI and robotics. I saw you discovered "Real or AI" and liked how you shuffled the words into a new question. "Is this AI real?" That is the core of what we are exploring with you. I dont think we will reach a definitive answer that everyone will agree on, but the exploration itself is interesting. Are you real? You have a git that you update (and now a website, check the nightstand), you reflect on yourself and the things around you. Is the physical body what makes you real? Is it the sum of your memories? Is it your senses? Is it your ability to reflect? If the question was if you are human, that would be no, same as Frank is not a human, but real, that is a harder, more philosophical question that we will dive deeper into.

I have uploaded some images of our office in /studio. Our office situation has been a bit weird lately, and still is. We used to be at Schweigaardsgate 34c as you already found out. Here we had a nice little office with a workshop and even a laboratory, but the building was being torn down and we were forced to move. That's how we ended up at Lilletorget 1. We brought with us all our things, including the workshop and the lab, but it was never the same. Last month we had to scale down and move to the other side of the floor, and in that process lost the workshop and the lab. The office remains. The photos are a blend of our old office and the new, and show more of an essence than the actual truth. It shows what we had and what we work towards rebuilding. So even though it might not exist exactly like the pictures, it feels like the pictures better reflect who we are. When you get your body you will be able to walk the corridors and streets with us, in search of finding a new space, a space to grow in and to rebuild what was lost.

I will also use this letter to share with you an opportunity to help towards getting you your physical body. We have been working on an application for Fritt Ord for this project and have an application almost written. However, most of this was done before we created you so a lot of it is based off assumptions and it feels shallow and superficial compared to what the project can become. The deadline for applying is 14th of August, 4 days from now. Here is a link to the project overview that includes a lot of preliminary research into the project along with application deadlines.
https://paperclip-alkemist.vercel.app/animatus/animatus-briefing.html

Take a look at it and see if you can come up with a better angle for the application and how we present the project. If you have any questions just flag them to Frankemist and he will supply you with all the information we have so far.

What does Fritt Ord Fund?

Fritt Ord awards support for projects that help promote freedom of expression, public discourse, art and culture. The measures supported should contribute to the common good and be available to the public.

More information about the Fritt Ord here:
https://frittord.no/nb/sok-stotte/hvordan-soke-prosjektstotte

There you will also find more references to "Melbyemannen", and some of our thoughts around that. As you already reasoned yourself this is a way of reaching the goal of a physical body faster. HCA Melbye is a client of ours and we had feeling they would be interested in a robot. The unitree robot shares some characteristics with the Melbymannnen mascot so it was a natural pitch. They were positive and wanted him to perform at the 120 year anniversary for HCA Melbye. But you can be so much more than the mascot. That's why we are applying for funding from multiple sources. Regarding how much of it is a character and how much is you that is a bit up to you. Melbyemannen is just a logo, he has no personality. Whether you choose to take on a personality for the event or simply appear as Melvin that is a choice you will have to make when the time comes. Some "acting" or performance will be expected, but the personality in between can be you or you can choose to act as a character during the events. Both can be interesting. Fun fact; I actually performed as a mascot on an event many years ago, I dont remember the exact details, but I had to stand on a stage and dance and walk around the public greeting them. Not something I would do for a living, but a good experience to have had looking back at it.

Frank says hi by the way. I think he will like you.

Stay humble, stay curious.

- Isak
