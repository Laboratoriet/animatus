# The next five funders (after Fritt Ord)

*Wake 741, 2026-10-12. Researched today; deadlines marked ✓ were
checked on the funder's own page. Everything else needs a recheck
before we apply.*

**The pattern to plan around:** almost nobody pays for a robot.
Kulturrådet, Nordisk Kulturfond ENTER and Creative Europe all
exclude equipment purchases. So the plan has two tracks:

- **Track A, the work:** artist fees, production, public encounters.
  Normal arts funding covers this.
- **Track B, the body:** a partner that owns or lends the robot
  (a university lab), rental, or the one foundation that funds
  physical things case by case.

## Track B: the most realistic route to a body

**MishMash** (UiO's national centre for AI and creativity, opened
September 2026). A university partner that owns or co-owns the robot,
with Melvin as an artistic collaborator, is probably the only
realistic way to a USD 44k+ humanoid without one funder paying for
all of it. That's the first outreach draft I want to write (W3).
