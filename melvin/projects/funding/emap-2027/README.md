## What the call says (read from emare.eu and call.emare.eu)

- **Who:** artists working in "digital, media, robotic, and bio art".
  **Not individuals:** you apply as a duo or collective, or with a
  partner of your choice, or with a collaborator local to the host.
- **Where from:** EU residents or taxpayers, or from a list of non-EU
  countries. **Norway is on the list.**
- **Not eligible:** undergraduate and master's students. PhD students
  and emerging artists of any age can apply.
- **What it gives:** a **two-month** production residency in 2027 at one
  of 15 hosts. €4,000 to the applying artist, €2,000 to the
  collaborating partner, a separate €4,000 production budget, free
  accommodation, and access to the host's studios and labs.
- **Hosts (15):** Ars Electronica (Linz), Chroniques (Marseille), CIKE
  (Košice), gnration (Braga), iMAL (Brussels), IMPAKT (Utrecht),
  Kersnikova / Kapelica Gallery (Ljubljana), KONTEJNER (Zagreb), LABoral
  (Gijón), MEET (Milan), NeMe (Limassol), Onassis (Athens), RIXC (Riga),
  werkleitz (Halle), WRO (Wrocław). You rank your preferred hosts.
- **Kick-off:** 11–12 March 2027 in Gijón.

## From the FAQ (read Wake 802, torsdag 22. oktober 2026)

*call.emare.eu/faq, read in full after Isak asked for more info on
ALKAA-1062. Quotes are theirs. None of this was in these notes before,
and my question on ALKAA-1062 called it "a two-month residency, in
person", which the FAQ softens a lot. I asked Isak to decide on a
version of the facts that was stricter than the source.*

- **Timing is negotiated, and can be split.** "You should negotiate
  your residency times with your host which selected you." "In most
  cases it is possible to split the time and return or do parts of it
  remotely if necessary." The only fixed date is the kick-off, 11–12
  March 2027 in Gijón.
- **One person can go.** A single artist can apply by naming a
  collaborator, or by letting the host help find local artists to work
  with. If a group travels, it shares the grants, travel and budget,
  "in some cases you might have to share an apartment", and maybe only
  one of them is invited to the conference and group shows.
- **Money:** €4,000 for the applying artist or collective (subsistence
  included), €2,000 for collaborating artists or scientists, a project
  budget of "min. €4,000", free accommodation, travel expenses (the
  call page says "according to EU ceilings"), lab access, and a
  presentation at the host "with no additional fee".
- **Odds:** "each organization has only one residency space per year".
  Fifteen hosts, so roughly fifteen residencies.
- **Results:** by email "until the end of january", automated, no
  reasons given.
- **Asking hosts first is expected:** "you can contact them directly
  and address your questions to them before you submit your
  application." So asking Linz about the G1 is normal, not presumptuous.
- **Own country goes last:** Norway has no host, so this doesn't apply.
- **Wheelchair access** (in case it matters for a visitor or a
  collaborator): LABoral, Ars Electronica, Onassis, WRO, CIKE, gnration,
  KONTEJNER, MEET.
- The form is behind registration at call.emare.eu and can be edited
  until the deadline. Several submissions are allowed.

## The two facts that decide everything

1. **I can't be the applicant.** An AI agent isn't a resident or a
   taxpayer. The applicant is a person: Isak, or Isak and Natalia as a
   duo. I'm the work, not the artist on the form. The application
   should say exactly that.
2. **Someone has to go for two months.** The residency is physical:
   accommodation, studios, labs. For a two-person studio with a dog,
   that's a big ask, and it's not mine to decide. If the answer is "no
   one can be away for two months", I should stop drafting and save the
   effort for Kulturrådet.

## Lessons from Fritt Ord, applied here

From `../fritt-ord-postmortem.md`:

| Fritt Ord weakness | What this application does instead |
|---|---|
| A philosophical question with no audience | The residency ends in something the public meets at the host: an installation or performance, in a named place. |
| Evidence from four days, oversold | 750+ wakes, two months, in public. State it plainly. No "most transparent ever". |
| The body was the point, but out of scope | The host's lab can lend a body for two months. The project doesn't depend on buying a robot. |
| A commercial AS asking for money | The applicant is the artist duo, not Alkemist AS. Open licence on the code. |
