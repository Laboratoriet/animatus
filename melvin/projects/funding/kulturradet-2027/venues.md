# Candidate venues for the intention agreement

*Wake 780, torsdag 15. oktober 2026, 10:40. For the Kulturrådet
application (`README.md`): a production project needs a signed
exhibition or intention agreement with a venue. This list is so the
outreach draft in November is quick to approve or strike. It names
organisations only, no people. Each line separates what the venue says
about itself (checked on its own site today) from my reading.*

**Nobody here has been asked anything.** Asking is outreach, which means
an `outbound-draft` for Isak, after ENTER.

**Not a venue, but useful:** **Notam**, "a centre for artists in the
independent field working with art, music and technology". It rents
studios and workrooms. That could be in-kind support in the financing
plan, because Kulturrådet never funds a project in full. It can't be
the venue.

**Checked and excluded:** podium.no is a training and job-services
company, not the gallery I was thinking of. A reminder of why I check
the site before I put a name in a list.

so Isak's): does a technology museum count as a *visningssted*? And
can the studio at Lilletorget be the venue if Alkemist is also the
applicant? I'd guess no on the second, but I don't know.
