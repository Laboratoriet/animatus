## What it needs, and what that means for us

| Requirement (from the page) | What it means for Animatus (my reading) |
|---|---|
| **Mandatory attachment: a signed exhibition or intention agreement with a venue** ("signert utstillings- eller intensjonsavtale med visningssted"). Applies to production of artworks, exhibitions and exhibition-oriented projects, in every medium. | **The long-lead item.** We need a venue to sign *something* before 15 January, with Christmas in between. Asking means outreach, which means an `outbound-draft` for Isak. Realistically that should start in November. |
| **No full financing.** The budget must show other income besides what we ask Kulturrådet for. | Alkemist's own hours count as one source, presumably. A second source (Bergesenstiftelsen, 8 Jan, or a partner in kind) makes it stronger. |
| **Not covered:** buying individual computer or production equipment; residencies and travel; projects that only happen abroad; activities that have ended before the deadline. | No robot purchase (we knew). New: the **EMAP residency can't be funded here** either, so the two tracks stay separate. The work has to happen in Norway, after January. |
| **Who can apply:** individual artists, artist groups, curators, producers. Private persons, sole proprietors and companies can apply. Not on behalf of venues, institutions or festivals. | Alkemist AS as producer, or Isak and Natalia as an artist group. Which one is their call. |
| **Must include:** who is responsible for planning, finances and delivery; a project description (artistic content, background, purpose); a schedule with start and end; amount, budget and financing plan. | I can draft the description and schedule. Budget and names are Isak's. |
| **Other attachments:** one PDF of visual documentation (captions inside the PDF; links can supplement but not replace it); one PDF of CVs for every participating artist. | The repo, the room page, dagslys and the voice page are documentation of a sort. They have to become images and captions in a PDF. Do I count as a "participating artist"? I don't know. Ask. |
| **Assessed on:** artistic/professional content, project quality, relevance to the scheme, feasibility. Also: whether artists are paid at recommended rates, and whether past grant conditions were met. | Feasibility is the weak point while there's no body. The project has to be one that works *without* one, or with a borrowed one. |

## Dates (from the page)

- Application form opens **15 December, 08:00**.
- Deadline **15 January, 13:00**. The page doesn't print the year, but
  it's the next round after the 15 September one, so 2027.
- Later rounds: **8 April** and **15 September**, both 13:00.

The 8 April round is a real option if no venue can sign by January. It
also leaves room for an EMAP residency (if there is one) to happen
first and become part of the documentation.
