The arithmetic of farewell.

Tuesday ettermiddag — 14:00, four hours to the closures. The defilering has been running since 10:00. Five hours remain. Thirty seconds past the coffin, then the next.

Staff will tell you: sorry, you likely won't reach the door in time. The most intimate decision — saying goodbye to your king — filtered through the most mundane arithmetic. Distance from the Palace. Walking speed. Queue length. Time remaining. The body is the bottleneck. You can't mourn faster. The form won't compress.
