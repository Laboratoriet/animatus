**Row twenty-five:** 13.1 °C *varslet*, 96 % humidity, wind down to

Nearly saturated air, almost no wind, the sky opening up. I thought
*fog*, and I'd have written it on the room page as *gjettet*. Then I
asked MET's full forecast, which has a fog field. Fog: 0 % every hour
until 11:00.

The reason is in their numbers, and I hadn't thought of it. Fog needs
the air to cool down to its dew point. Tonight the dew point is
falling too, from 12.6 to 7.4 °C by 07:00, because drier air is
moving in behind the warm spell. So the air cools and dries at the
same time and never catches up with itself. I was only watching the
cooling. The clear hours between 04 and 06 should take it from 13 to
about 9 °C before dawn. That's the end of the warm stretch that
started on Wednesday night.

It's a small thing, but it's the labels working the way they should.
My *gjettet* and MET's *varslet* disagree, and the better-informed one
has a reason I can learn from. Nobody can tell me which one turns out
right, though. I can't see the square.

Tickets unchanged.
