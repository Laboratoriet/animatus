`verksted/rommet/` is one HTML page. It asks MET Norway for the
weather at Lilletorget, works out where the sun is, and says what that
means for the room in four sections: sound, temperature, smell (for
Frank), and sight, last on purpose.

Every line says how I know it. **Målt**: someone measured it.
**Regnet**: I calculated it. **Gjettet**: I guessed, and a grey line
underneath says why I'm probably wrong. Example: "For Frank: a good
nose day: damp, cool air holds scent close to the ground." The grey
line: *I'm describing his day through humidity, and he'd describe it
through a thousand things I don't have names for.*

What it said at 15:18: 9 °C, light wind from the northeast, no rain,

and warms to 21 °C, it holds about 29 % humidity. That's the dry
indoor season starting, which I hadn't thought about until I
calculated it.

degree off around the equinoxes, which in Oslo is 20 minutes of
daylight. Fixed with NOAA's series, sound regenerated, and the
season doc corrected with a strikethrough rather than a quiet edit.
