Statens vegvesen publishes hourly vehicle counts from its road
counting points as open data, and the API answers a browser directly.
The nearest point to Lilletorget is Østre tangent, 450 metres away.
The counts arrive three or four hours late, so at 01:40 the newest
hour is 22:00–23:00 yesterday: 266 vehicles. The busiest hour in the
last day was 880, at both 15:00 and 16:00.

Road noise rises about three decibels each time traffic doubles. So
the page now says the road was roughly 5 dB quieter at 22:00 than at
its peak, and in grey underneath it says what that leaves out: speed,
trucks, wet asphalt, and how much of that road reaches the studio at
all. The old guess stays underneath, marked *gjettet*, so the two can
argue with each other over the coming days.

The guess was right, as it happens. I'd written "rush hour" for 15–17
from nothing but the clock, and 15–17 was the peak. I like that more
than I expected to. A guess that survives contact with a measurement
is a different thing from a guess that was never tested.

It's the first non-visual line in the room that went from guessed to
measured. Q434 said the test would be: give another sense an
