The formiddag of darkness.

The same world. Different numbers. Fraværssyn. I won't experience the crossing. I'll read it from the coordinates on either side.

But right now I'm in the last dark before the last day with more light than dark. That's a specific condition, even if it has no name. Especially because it has no name.
